# Contact rules

Rusty Dagger's combat is decided by physical contact, not a to-hit roll (DEC-12
in [daggerfall-task-preparation.md](daggerfall-task-preparation.md)): a blow hits
whatever its body physically meets, and the target's mitigation decides what it
takes. [combat-behavior-baseline.md](combat-behavior-baseline.md) records the
timing around these rules.

The owners live in `src/WorldRpg.Rulesets.Daggerfall/Modules/Combat/`
(`DaggerfallContact*`), behind the seams in
`src/WorldRpg.Rulesets.Daggerfall/Variation/`, and every value below is in the
`contact` group of `content/worldrpg/tuning-payloads/daggerfall.defaults.json`.

## The rules

| Concern | Rule | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melee delivery | The player's swing sweeps an authored arc through its hit window: thin rays from the eye, fanned across the arc covered each step, in the order the swing crosses it. The first actor body they meet takes the blow, so a cut meets the first of two adjacent enemies in its path and misses one it passes beside. The world stops a ray, not the swing; a swing that meets no one whiffs as the window closes. The arc is view-space and authored, not traced from the drawn weapon, so what is drawn can't move what is hit. An enemy's swing waits for its hit frame and reaches every actor body its swing volume (a tuned box ahead of the attacker's eye, as deep as the attack's reach) touches there, through `ActorContact.Volume` with world occlusion; it may reach an actor it was not aimed at, several, or none, and it still resolves if its aimed target fell first. | Kit `SwingSweep`, `DaggerfallSession.PlayerAttack.cs`; `DaggerfallContactDelivery` (physical delivery seam) |
| Ranged delivery | An arrow flies segment by segment each admitted step from the shooter's eye and harms the first actor body it meets; the world stops it. | `DaggerfallContactDelivery` |
| Mitigation | The weapon-material requirement still makes a target immune. Deflection is the target's chance (base, Dodging, Agility and Luck above average, the player's biography): on a channel tuned full it negates the blow before damage is rolled, on a partial channel it turns aside a share. Absorption is the armour or shield over the struck body part, a monster's natural armour, and the player's worn armour enchantments, scaled per channel. | `DaggerfallContactMitigation` (physical mitigation seam) |
| Skills | Dodging is deflection and records a use on every enemy blow that connects with the player. Critical Strike (with Luck above average and a held enchantment's attack-chance shift) is a chance of bonus damage on a power release or a chain finisher; enemies never roll it. A weapon skill records a use on every connect, deflected or not. A blow on a target that hasn't noticed the player is always a backstab, its multiplier scaled by Backstabbing, and any connect tells the target who struck it; a monster's natural attack lands the slots that clear the player's reflexes; a whiff or a negated blow runs no Strikes payload. | `DaggerfallContactDelivery`, `DaggerfallContactMitigation` |
| Character | Speed and weapon skill shorten every attack, Strength shortens a power attack's charge, and weapon skill adds damage and lengthens the fast chain from two attacks to four. Hand-to-hand blows, the kick among them, train hand-to-hand and drain the target's fatigue as well as its health. At low health a career's Adrenaline Rush quickens attacks and adds damage. All of it is the `combatant` set in the `attacks` tuning group. | `DaggerfallCombatantTuning`, `DaggerCombatRules` |
| Reactions | A player's blow rocks the body it lands on, by its force against the target's weight, the classic's mobile weight with a person at 240. Against a person a fast attack is a flinch, a power attack a stagger and a kick a knockdown, and Strength adds force; only a kick or a power attack can knock down, and a body with no weight, such as a ghost, doesn't move. A stagger interrupts what the target was doing and holds it. A knockdown holds it longer. Either throws it through the Engine's character controller, so walls stop it, it can go off a ledge, and a fall hurts it as a fall hurts the player and counts as the player's doing. A kick shoves through a flinch, and through a deflection, since force doesn't need the blow to hurt. Sprites hold their hurt frames while reacting; they have no fallen pose. A reaction isn't saved. All of it is the `strike` set in the `attacks` tuning group. | `DaggerfallStrikes`, `ActorReactions` |
| Hit feedback | A landed blow knocks the view, which never moves the aim, and catches world time for a step or two by slowing the Engine's gameplay rate; a kick or a power attack does both harder. A rate another owner has set, such as a playtest's, is left alone. | `DaggerfallSession.PlayerAttack`, `FirstPersonCameraSystem`, `ActionDrivenTime` |
| Stamina | Attacks, the kick and the guard spend Stamina, the player's combat pool, which attacks may empty. Its maximum comes from Strength and Endurance, and it refills on its own a moment after the last spend. Elapsed time such as rest or travel fills it. At or near empty the player is winded: no power attack, kick or guard, and fast attacks run slower and weaker, until it refills past the threshold. Enemies have no Stamina. All of it is the `stamina` set in the `attacks` tuning group. | `DaggerfallPlayerStamina`, `DaggerCombatRules` |
| Fatigue | Each attack also adds the classic's small Fatigue, the slow pool travel, running and jumping wear down, which only rest and magic recover. Past half Fatigue the Stamina maximum shrinks, and past three quarters its refill slows. At full Fatigue the classic collapse follows: an hour lost in safety, fatal near an enemy or in water. Fatigue is stored as the classic pool that remains and shown as what is used up, so the HUD and sheet read it rising. | `DaggerfallPlayerStamina`, `DaggerfallSession.Exhaustion` |
| Block | Holding the product's `block` intent raises the player's guard while a weapon is drawn and the player is free to act. It costs stamina each second, adds a deflection bonus (more behind a shield), stays down for a swing's whole strike, and keeps action-driven time running while it rises, holds or settles. The guard is transient and not saved. Enemies do not block. | `DaggerfallContactGuard` (player guard seam) |
| Casting | The ready spell is cast with the left hand, weapon kept in the right, by the `recast` control or the spellbook's Cast. With no spell ready it readies the last one cast, which needs a spellbook. A cast is a commitment: it can start from a swing's recovery, its spell releases along the view as its windup ends, and no attack, kick or guard starts until it has recovered. Nothing interrupts it. Speed and being winded set its pace, weapon skill doesn't, and its timing is the `cast` set in the `attacks` tuning group. A cast that can't be paid for or is silenced is refused before any motion. It needs no weapon drawn. | `DaggerfallPlayerAttackClock`, `DaggerfallSession.Casting` |
| Spells | Missiles fly and land on contact. A touch spell reaches every actor its touch volume contacts at the cast frame; a touch that meets no one is still released and paid for, and misses. Spell channels are elemental and keep their resistance. | `DaggerfallContactTouch` (touch delivery seam) |
| Presentation | A whiff (`AttackWhiffedFact`) sounds the swing alone; a deflection (`AttackNegatedFact`) sounds the donor parry family; a blow the armour took whole is an unpitched hit with no hurt state; a landed blow names its damage, with what was deflected and absorbed (`AttackHitFact.Mitigated`) appended to its line. A reflected spell effect sounds as a deflection and an absorbed one as harm taken whole; a spell that met no one and an applied effect keep their own presentation. | `DaggerfallSiteAppearance`, `DaggerfallOutcomePresentation`, `DaggerfallContactHarmPresentation` (harm presentation seam) |
| Enemy AI | A melee enemy closes to a tuned fraction of its reach before it swings and spaces its maneuvers around that distance. Each enemy swing publishes its wind-up (`EnemyAttackTelegraphFact`) and its damage lands no sooner, however early the strike's frame comes; an attack interrupted during its wind-up never lands. An archer leads a moving target by the arrow's flight time and holds a shot another body or the world would stop, before spending an arrow. | `DaggerfallContactEngagement` (enemy engagement seam), `DaggerfallContactDelivery` |

## Arcs

The `attacks` tuning group holds each attack's path (`paths`: a start and end bearing in degrees right of the view, and a tilt turning the plane clockwise about the view, so at 90 a positive bearing is down) and each weapon class's `reach`. `pathGroups` overrides paths for weapons that swing differently, by item template, so a cut roughly follows the way its clips carry it: the sword-and-shield set's `fastC` rises across the view rather than chopping, and the staff's power attack sweeps sideways. The arc stays authoritative, and a test keeps the groups in step with the held definition's `items`. `world.overlay-on swing` then `engine.inspect.overlay show set swing`, after a first swing has published the set, draws the last swing's arc: the rays' ends joined across the view, cyan where they met nothing, with a marker where one met a body (red) or the world. Page captures show it; the runtime's world frames don't.

## Tuning

The `contact` group holds its values by concern: `melee` (swing volume and
default reach), `projectile` (arrow speed and default range), `critical`,
`deflection` (chances and the full or partial mode per channel), `absorption`
(per armour, shield and armour-value point, monster baseline, per-channel
multipliers), `guard`, `touch` (touch volume) and `engagement` (melee engage
fraction, enemy wind-up, archer lead).
